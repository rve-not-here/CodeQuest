Rules behind these amounts

How XP is earned and spent. These rules are not additional transactions.

Mission completion
Awards that mission's configured points, once. Re-submitting a finished mission cannot farm it again.
Knowledge Check
Awards no XP. Results record your understanding.
Boss Challenge
A qualifying first pass awards 100 XP, once per course. Later retries never re-award or reverse it.
Wrong submission
Deducts up to 10 XP per failed submission, stopping at zero.
Hints
Cost 5, 10, then 15 XP for the first, second, and third hint.
Solution reveal
Costs 30 XP. It does not reduce the mission's reward, which is still awarded on completion.
Floor
A balance never falls below zero. A deduction at the floor is recorded, clamped to zero.
