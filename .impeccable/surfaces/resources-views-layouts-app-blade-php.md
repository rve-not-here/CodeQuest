## Scope

Operate mode. Replace the shared application shell and design system across student, teacher, and admin routes — plus the auth and landing surfaces — with the freeCodeCamp interface grammar, without changing behavior, content, or role boundaries.

## Direction contract

THESIS: CodeQuest is freeCodeCamp, cloned: a dark navy top bar over calm white surfaces, flat blue actions, hairline borders, and Lato/Roboto Mono. It refuses the retired retro-terminal field manual.

OWN-WORLD: Navy #0a0a23 header and editor; white #ffffff pages and cards; #198eee primary actions, links, and progress; #d0d0d5 hairlines; #2a2a40 body text on #f5f6f7 alt surfaces; square controls; Lato 400/700/900 for UI and headings; Roboto Mono for code.

STORY: Every role lands on a legible campus: navigate from the top bar, read state in bordered white cards and dense tables, act through flat blue buttons. Data-heavy teacher and admin pages keep their tables, restyled to the same tokens.

FIRST VIEWPORT (login): split hero on white. Left: Lato 900 navy headline and sub-copy with a blue call-to-action. Right: bordered white sign-in card. Session surfaces adopt the top-nav shell with a drawer on mobile.

FORM: freeCodeCamp Clone — user-pinned replacement world (full clone, no roll).

SIGNATURE: The mission challenge keeps its three-pane workspace: dark navy pane strip over the oneDark editor and white preview, actions in a fixed bottom toolbar.
